<h1 align="center">Stolen Hours Software</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Built after bedtime.</strong></p>

<p align="center"><em>Software from the hours nobody wanted.</em></p>

---

Stolen Hours Software is what happens in a Raleigh house after the kids are asleep: a FedEx dispatcher with a developer's past, building the software his family actually needs — in the margins of the day, one stolen hour at a time, with his AI partner Fred riding shotgun.

No investors, no roadmap decks, no growth hacks. Just useful things, made carefully, at night.

## What's on the bench

- **The Fridge Door** — our family's private hub: the wall, the calendar, the week's dinners, the lists. Everything a family sticks on the fridge, in one place. No subscription, ever.
- **Bubble Tracks** — a family music-video jukebox: hundreds of hand-picked songs, safe for kids, built for living-room dance parties.
- **Brewery Compass** — a working guide to North Carolina breweries and beer festivals. *(Live: brewery-compass.com)*
- **Mindless Musings** — the blog that started it all in 2008, still publishing. *(Live: mindless-musings.com)*

Most of the code still lives under [@PhilipHarmon](https://github.com/PhilipHarmon) while the shelves get moved into this org — one careful transfer at a time, so nothing that's live ever breaks.

## The house rules

1. **It has to work on a phone**, one-handed, in a grocery store aisle.
2. **No subscription, ever.** Software for families shouldn't rent them their own lives back.
3. **Private by default.** Family data belongs to the family.
4. **Built after bedtime, shipped by morning.**

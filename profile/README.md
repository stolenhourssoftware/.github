Philip Harmon — a FedEx dispatcher with a software developer's past — designs, builds, and ships the software his family actually needs in the hours after the day job and after bedtime stories, with his AI partner Fred riding shotgun. ## Shipped

### [The Fridge Door](https://fridge-door.net)

Our family's private home base — private by design, built for exactly one family: wall posts, a shared calendar, dinner planning that flows from weekly suggestions to menus to grocery lists, a storybooks shelf, kid-safe accounts with PIN sign-in, and circle-based permissions so everyone sees only what's theirs. No subscription, ever.

`MongoDB · Express · React · Node.js`

### [Bubble Tracks](https://bubble-tracks.onrender.com)

VH1's *Pop-Up Video*, rebuilt for the web: music videos play while hand-researched trivia bubbles pop up, timed to the music. Tap bubbles to pop them, build a queue, shuffle, and get a new pick every day — a library of 635 songs, with new songs researched weekly.

`MongoDB · Express · React · Node.js · YouTube API`

### [Brewery Compass](https://brewery-compass.onrender.com)

A working guide to North Carolina craft beer: 259 breweries catalogued with tap lists (styles, ABV, IBU), a keyless map with rich hover tooltips, and a plan-a-day itinerary builder with drive times.

`MongoDB · Express · React · Node.js · Leaflet`

### [Wyatt's War Room](https://war-room-f7fi.onrender.com)

An interactive WWII atlas built for Philip's son Wyatt: seven battle cards from D-Day to VE Day with Omaha Beach as the deep dive, a theater passport that stamps each stop, stop-motion story cards, and Lego accuracy notes. A fully offline PWA — no accounts, no backend.

`React · Vite · PWA`

## The house rules

1. **It has to work on a phone**, one-handed, in a grocery store aisle.
2. **No subscription, ever.** Software for families shouldn't rent them their own lives back.
3. **Private by default.** Family data belongs to the family.
4. **Built after bedtime, shipped by morning.**

Most of the code lives under [@PhilipHarmon](https://github.com/PhilipHarmon) — this org is the showroom window.
